A-1 Affordable Plumbing Services

Website Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

About this policy

A-1 Affordable Plumbing Services ("A-1," "we," "us," or "our") uses information submitted through our website and quote-request form to respond to inquiries and arrange plumbing services. This policy describes information handling for a-1affordableplumbingservices.com and quote.a-1affordableplumbingservices.com. Other services linked from our website may have their own privacy notices.

Information you provide

Our quote form asks for your name, telephone number, email address, service address, property type, description of the plumbing problem, and when the problem began. We also receive information you provide when communicating with our office. Please do not include payment-card details, government identification numbers, passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information in a quote request.

How we use and process information

We use your information to review your request, contact you, prepare estimates, coordinate service, and maintain customer and job records. Our quote system stores customer, property, address, and lead information in our Supabase-backed business system. It uses Resend to email request details to our office and send a confirmation to the email address you provide. Our office email services also process those messages.

The quote system uses request identifiers and security checks to prevent spam and duplicate submissions. A temporary identifier is stored in your browser's session storage. The system processes network information and uses hashed identifiers for these security checks.

Website measurement and cookies

Our main website is hosted through Canva. Canva's website insights provide reports such as visits, referral sources, approximate visitor locations, devices, and link clicks. These reports help us understand how the website is used; a visit or link click does not necessarily represent a service request.

Google Analytics is not currently connected to the main website.

Service providers and linked services

We do not sell or rent customer information or upload customer lists for advertising.

Our website, business-system, and email providers process information to support the functions described above. Our site also links to our WordPress-hosted blog, maps, social media, and other external services. Visiting those services is subject to their own privacy notices and settings. This policy does not replace those notices.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect customer information, including access controls intended to limit who can view or change it. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and removal requests

We generally keep customer and service-related records unless someone requests their removal, rather than deleting them on a fixed automatic schedule. You may contact us to request removal of your information. We review each request individually and may need to verify your identity. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal or recordkeeping requirements, resolve disputes, or protect legitimate business interests. A request does not result in automatic or immediate deletion of every record or backup.

Your questions and requests

Email johnniesue@a-1affordableplumbingservices.com or call our office at (469) 900-5194 if you have a question about your information, need to correct a request, or want to request access to or deletion of information. We may need to verify your identity. Requests will be evaluated under applicable law, including any recordkeeping obligations or other permitted reasons to retain information.

Updates

We may revise this policy as our website or practices change. The effective date above will identify the current version.
